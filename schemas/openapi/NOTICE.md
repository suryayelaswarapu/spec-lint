# Official OpenAPI JSON Schemas

`oas-3.0.json` (id `https://spec.openapis.org/oas/3.0/schema/2021-09-28`) and
`oas-3.1.json` (id `https://spec.openapis.org/oas/3.1/schema/2022-10-07`) are the official
schemas published by the OpenAPI Initiative in
[OAI/OpenAPI-Specification](https://github.com/OAI/OpenAPI-Specification), licensed under the
Apache License 2.0. They are vendored unmodified, so the Java and Go implementations can check
structural validity offline (`oas3-valid-schema`) without fetching anything at runtime.
